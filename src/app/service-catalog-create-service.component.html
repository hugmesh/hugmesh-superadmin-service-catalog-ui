Create a service

Stand up a brand-new service from scratch: pick an identity and a starter archetype, choose a source, optionally federate its UI, then apply the chain. Each step runs against the real admin-bff wizard and surfaces its own result — nothing is faked.

Ships dark: the provision effector and the federation registration both ship dark by default (no real ArgoCD apply, no real manifest registration) — the wizard records the intent and reports each leg honestly. Scaffolding and federating each require a fresh step-up.
