Add a service

Provision a recorded deployment into a real service through the Service Catalog effector — a per-service role, a route-access rule, and an ArgoCD application. Pick the domain, pick the deployment, then choose one of the four sources.

The effector ships dark by default — provisioning wires the per-service role and route-access rule and records the source; the real ArgoCD apply lands when the effector is enabled. Record a deployment first in the Deployments view.
